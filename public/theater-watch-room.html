How this room works

Big-screen stream: Paste a YouTube livestream or video URL and it loads on the theater screen.
Immersive POV: Look left and right from your seat, switch between dark cinema and house lighting, or enter fullscreen cinema mode. You are watching from your own seat.
Real attendance: Signed-in M4D members take a seat. Seats update live for everyone when members join and leave.
Privacy: Only members who tap Join Room appear in the theater. Leaving or disconnecting removes their seat. Other viewers may see your M4D username and avatar.
M4D Theater live audience. Presence tracks connected members in this room, not YouTube viewer statistics. Your livestream continues playing through the official YouTube embedded player.
