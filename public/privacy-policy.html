Privacy Policy

How Marriage4Dummies-Reacts handles information when you use our community website, member features, and YouTube-connected Theater Chat Tablet.

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Who we are. “Marriage4Dummies-Reacts” (also called “M4D” or “we”) operates the community website at marriage4dummies-reacts.netlify.app, including its Theater watch room. Contact us about privacy at devc122969@gmail.com.

Our Theater uses YouTube API Services to help participating members view and post messages in a livestream’s real YouTube live chat. Using the Theater is not the same as giving M4D your Google password.

1. Information collected through our website

Depending on which features you choose, the website may process:

  • Account and profile information: your sign-in email, username, avatar, banner, profile biography, preferences and account identifiers.
  • Community content: posts, comments, reactions, friend requests, private or group messages, and content or images you choose to submit.
  • Safety information: reports, blocks, moderation decisions and relevant records needed to review abuse or support requests.
  • Feature activity: watch-list entries, favorites, seat or presence status, notifications, and any song-request submissions or supporting screenshots.
  • Technical information: browser/device information, authentication/session details, and logs necessary for hosting, troubleshooting, and security; third-party services may also collect information under their own policies.

Some profile and community details are displayed to other members according to the features you use. Messages you send to public channels or a public livestream are not private.

2. Google account access and YouTube API Services

If you select Connect YouTube, Google shows an authorization screen and asks you to grant permissions. The Theater currently requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl scope, which can authorize more YouTube account actions than live-chat posting. The feature's intended use is to identify the connected YouTube account as needed for chat, read eligible livestream chat messages, and submit a message only when you press Send.

The Theater may access a livestream or chat identifier, live-chat messages and associated public author/channel information, and the Google/YouTube authorization token needed to make approved YouTube API requests. Some of that information is supplied directly by Google or YouTube rather than by the M4D account system.

When you send a message: your message is submitted to YouTube on behalf of the Google/YouTube account you authorized, and may be publicly visible in the livestream chat. YouTube manages that message according to its own policies and the livestream’s chat settings.

We do not ask you to disclose your Google password to the M4D website. Google handles the account sign-in and permission prompt. We do not sell Google user data or use it to serve targeted advertising. Google user data is used to provide the user-requested YouTube-connected feature, maintain its security, and comply with applicable requirements—not for unrelated purposes.

You can review or withdraw the app’s Google access at Google Account third-party connections. Revoking access stops future authorized API requests but does not automatically erase messages already published on YouTube.

Use of this feature is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service; by using the YouTube-connected features, you agree to be bound by those terms. Please also see the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

We use information as needed to operate the features members request: account authentication, community and profile displays, friend and messaging tools, notifications, theater participation and live chat, moderation, customer support, and site security. Information may also be used to diagnose errors and maintain service reliability. We do not use Google authorization to publish YouTube messages without the user taking the relevant send action.

4. Information sharing and service providers

Information you intentionally post in public community areas or YouTube live chat may be visible to other people. Messages sent through private or group features are intended for their participants, subject to safety, technical and moderation needs.

Our website uses external technology providers for hosting and selected features. These may include Netlify (hosting and deployment), Supabase (accounts, data and realtime features), Google/YouTube (login authorization, video and live chat), and, where enabled, OneSignal (notifications) and LiveKit (calling). Their handling of data is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We may disclose information when necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, prevent abuse or protect users and the service. We do not sell personal information or Google user data.

5. Choices, access, and deletion

You may choose not to connect YouTube, not to submit public chat messages, and not to use optional community features. Where available, you can update profile details, remove content, adjust notifications, and use blocking or reporting controls.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by M4D, email devc122969@gmail.com with a description of your request and enough information to verify the account. We will review requests in line with applicable law and any necessary security or recordkeeping obligations. We may not be able to remove copies of public YouTube chat messages from YouTube; use YouTube's own tools and policies for those messages.

Disconnecting Google access is handled through your Google Account settings. If you ask us to delete any Google-derived data that we retain, we will review and act on the request according to applicable obligations.

6. Retention, safeguards, and third-party services

We retain account and community information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the related features, address abuse or support issues, and meet legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it when appropriate. Exact retention periods depend on the type of information and service involved. You may contact us to request details or deletion.

We use HTTPS and service-provider access controls to help protect information. No website or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not share passwords, Google authorization tokens, or sensitive personal information in public chat or community posts.

Embedded videos, Google authorization, and other third-party features may connect your browser directly to external services; review their respective privacy notices for more information.

7. Children and audience settings

The community and YouTube live-chat features are intended for people eligible to use the relevant services under their applicable terms. We do not intentionally request personal information from children who are not permitted to use these services. If you believe a child has submitted personal information contrary to applicable requirements, contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as features or data practices change. We will update the effective date above, and provide additional notice or request consent where required. Material changes to our handling of Google user data will be disclosed before using that data for new purposes.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, email devc122969@gmail.com.

Transparency note: This policy describes the intended operation of our website and YouTube Chat Tablet. If a feature's actual storage, sharing or retention behavior differs, this notice must be updated to match before public launch.
